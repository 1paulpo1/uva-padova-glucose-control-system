── CLINICAL INTERPRETATION ──

  Parameters that CAN be estimated from CGM alone (kp2, k1, k2, Ts, 
  ka1, ki, p2U) are globally identifiable. This includes:
    • kp2 (glucose effectiveness on EGP) — key for insulin titration
    • k1/k2 (glucose transport rates) — microvascular function
    • p2U (insulin action lag) — relevant for closed-loop tuning
    • ki (EGP delay) — hepatic insulin resistance marker
    • ka1 (SC insulin absorption) — pump therapy parameter

  Parameters that require additional data (meal info, dual-tracer, etc.):
    • Vm0, Vmx, Km0 (utilization Michaelis-Menten) — need glucose clamp
    • kp1, kp3 (EGP amplitude) — confounded with VG, Fcns
    • VG (glucose volume) — requires tracer dilution
    • Fcns (CNS uptake) — known constant in practice

  The identifiable ratios Vm0/kp3, VG/kp3, (Fcns−kp1)/kp3 suggest that
  fixing any one of {kp3, VG, Fcns, kp1} to a literature value would
  break the scaling symmetry and make the others identifiable.
