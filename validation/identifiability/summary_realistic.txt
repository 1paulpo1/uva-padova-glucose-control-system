── CLINICAL SUMMARY ──

  From CGM + pump + meals only (no decomposition):

  15/26 parameters are locally identifiable:
    Ts, k1, k2, ka1, ka2, kabs, kd, kgri, ki, kmax, kp2, m1, m2, m4, p2U

  11 parameters are NOT locally identifiable (scaling symmetry group):
    BW, Fcns, Ib, Km0, VG, VI, Vm0, Vmx, f, kp1, kp3
    — these form 5 scaling pairs and Km0 (MM term)

  What fixes the scaling group (greedy, from prior_fix_experiment):
    Fix Km0 + VI + BW + Fcns + Ib (5 population priors)
    → all 21 remaining become locally identifiable

  Practical takeaway:
    With CGM data and population priors for just 5 parameters,
    the remaining 21 individual parameters are estimable.
    11 structural N parameters are exactly the scaling pairs —
    these need either an additional measurement (Ip) or population prior(s).
